Features

What Synder takes off your plate

Plain results first. The accounting detail is one click away.

Sales post to your books on their own

Orders, fees, refunds and taxes from 30+ platforms land in your accounting software, in the right currency.

For accountants

Per transaction (each sale with SKU and customer) or Summary (one journal entry per day, month or payout).

Payouts that match your ledger

Synder compares what each platform paid out with what is in your books and flags differences before month end.

For accountants

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes run through a clearing account that should reach zero after each payout. Every reconciled period is logged for audit.

Every transaction in the right account

Set if-then rules once. Synder applies them to each transaction as it syncs.

For accountants

Rules match on amount, vendor, description or location and assign accounts, classes and tax codes.

Subscription revenue in the right month

Synder RevRec spreads each payment over the months it covers and follows upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

For accountants

Builds ASC 606 recognition schedules from Stripe data or an Excel upload.

Your numbers on one dashboard

Synder Insights shows sales, product and customer reports for every connected platform.

For accountants

KPI reports per platform, updated daily.

Set up the way your books work

Rename line items, pull in metadata, remove taxes or map transactions your own way.

For accountants

Custom transaction mapping for high-volume setups.

Getting started

How it works

Most setups take an afternoon, not a project.

1

Connect

Link your sales channels and payment tools to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct. Past sales can be imported too.

2

Choose the detail

Post each sale on its own, or one summary entry per day, month or payout.

3

Let it run

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes post as they happen, sorted by your rules.

4

Review exceptions

Check only what Synder flags, then close the month.
