How transaction reconciliation works

It compares every platform row to your books so you catch missing sales, fees, and amount mismatches before they throw off your close.
You choose when to run it. Synder matches rows for that period and surfaces exceptions — you stay in control of when the check happens.
No. Reconciliation only compares data already synced to Synder — it doesn’t consume sync credits.
No. The check is read-only. It never posts, edits, or deletes anything in your accounting system.
There’s nothing to undo — results are a report of matches and gaps. Re-run anytime with a different period or after you fix books.
