Platform rows ↔ Books

› Is this the same as matching payouts?
No. Payout matching ties deposits to the bank. Transaction reconciliation checks each platform sale and fee against your clearing account, line by line.
› Will it change anything in my books?
No. Running reconciliation is read-only — nothing is posted or changed in your books.
