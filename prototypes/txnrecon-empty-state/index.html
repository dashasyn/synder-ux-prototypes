Common questions

› Why should I use it?
It catches missing sales, fee mismatches, and amount-off rows before you close the period — so your clearing account and reports stay accurate.
› Automated reconciliation
Synder compares platform activity to your clearing account line by line and groups issues into Matched, Missing, and Amount off for you to review.
› No sync balance changes
Reconciliation is a read-only comparison. It does not post, edit, or sync anything to your books or change sync balances.
› Will it change anything in my books?
No. Running reconciliation is read-only — nothing is posted or changed in your books.
› Can I undo and re-run a reconciliation?
Yes. Because nothing is written to your books, you can re-run any period anytime. Prior check results are replaced by the new run.
› Is this the same as matching payouts?
No. Payout matching ties deposits to bank transfers. Transaction reconciliation checks every platform row against your clearing account line by line.
› How is this different from balance reconciliation?
Balance reconciliation confirms ending balances agree. This finds the individual missing, amount-off, and duplicate rows that cause a gap.
