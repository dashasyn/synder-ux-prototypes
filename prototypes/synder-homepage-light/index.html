What Synder does

The bookkeeping work you no longer do by hand

Four jobs Synder takes over. Each one starts with the result. The accounting detail is one click away.

Your sales show up in your books on their own

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes from 30+ platforms post to your accounting software automatically. Pick how much detail you want to see.

  • Every sale, fee, refund and tax, from every channel
  • Past sales can be imported too
  • Multiple currencies handled

Know your books match what you were paid

Synder checks what your platforms recorded against what is in your books, and flags anything that doesn't line up before month end.

  • Mismatches flagged early, not found at close
  • Every reconciled period is logged for audit
For accountants: how it works
Each sale, fee, refund and tax syncs to a clearing account. Synder then reconciles both sides inside the app, and its matching engine flags discrepancies.

Every transaction goes to the right account

Set simple if-then rules once. Synder applies them to every transaction as it syncs, across all your platforms.

  • Rules by amount, vendor, description or location
  • No more sorting transactions one by one
For accountants: what rules can set
Rules assign accounts, classes and tax codes to each transaction during sync.
RulesSample
If vendor is Facebook Adsthen account Advertising
If location is Austin storethen class Retail · Austin
If description contains "wholesale"then account Wholesale sales
If amount is over $5,000then tax code Exempt

Subscription revenue counted in the right month

For subscription businesses: Synder spreads each payment over the months it was earned, and keeps up with upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

  • Works from Stripe data or an Excel upload
  • Revenue recognized monthly on your P&L
