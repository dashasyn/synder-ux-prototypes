Invoice date New Upgrade to use
Chooses which month the invoice (and its revenue) lands in your books. Created date = when Stripe created the invoice. Issued date (Date of issue on the Stripe PDF) = when the invoice was finalized for the customer.

New connections default to Issued date. Existing connections stay on Created date until you change them. Already-synced invoices are not re-dated.

This feature is available on higher plans.
You cannot change Invoice date while revenue recognition starts from an invoice date.

Cancel sync if there is no matching open invoice found for a payment
If no matching open invoice is found, Synder cancels the payment sync.
Sync unpaid (open) invoices
Synder syncs open invoices to your books. When the payment arrives, it is attached to the matching invoice and the invoice is marked as paid.
Sync zero invoices
Synder imports and syncs invoices where every line and the total are 0.

Sales recording date Upgrade to use
Chooses which date Synder uses when recording Stripe invoice sales in summaries. Invoice created date = when Stripe created the invoice. Invoice issued date (Date of issue on the Stripe PDF) = when the invoice was finalized. Payment date = when the customer paid.

Invoice created date and Payment date are available on all plans. Invoice issued date requires Pro or higher.
