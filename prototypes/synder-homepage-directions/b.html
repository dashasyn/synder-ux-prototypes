Features

The whole sales-to-ledger loop, handled

Plain results up front. The accounting detail sits underneath.

01 / SYNC

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes post themselves

Choose Per transaction (each sale with SKU and customer) or Summary (one journal entry per day, month or payout).

Picture: Table: synced transactions by source, type and amount with posted status
02 / RECONCILE

Payouts that match, or a flag that says why not

Everything runs through a clearing account that should end at zero.

Picture: Mini card: matched payouts and one mismatch
03 / CATEGORIZE

If this, then that account

Rules by amount, vendor, description or location set accounts, classes and tax codes.

Picture: Rule builder: two if-then rows
04 / REVREC

Subscription revenue in the right month

Synder RevRec builds ASC 606 recognition schedules from Stripe data or an Excel upload, and follows upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

Picture: Timeline: one annual payment recognized month by month

Synder Insights
