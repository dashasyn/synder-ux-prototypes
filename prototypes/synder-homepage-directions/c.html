Ecommerce and retail

Stop copying orders into your books

Every sale, fee, refund and tax from your stores and payment tools shows up in your ledger, categorized the way you want.

  • Sales from 30+ platforms, including Shopify, Amazon, PayPal and Square
  • Smart rules put each transaction in the right account
  • Anything that does not match is flagged before month end

For accountants: Per transaction (each sale with SKU and customer) or Summary (one journal entry per day, month or payout). Each sale goes through a clearing account for reconciliation.

480+ hours saved a year on categorizing at PlayYourCourt, plus $24K+ on bookkeeping services.

SaaS and subscriptions

Count subscription revenue in the right month

Synder RevRec spreads each payment over the months it covers and adjusts for upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

  • Works from Stripe data or an Excel upload
  • Revenue shown monthly on your P&L
  • Books stay ready for audit

For accountants: RevRec builds ASC 606 recognition schedules.

95% less manual reconciliation time at Stape, with 180 hours saved a year.
