How it works, in four steps.

Connect your sales channels and ledger

Link Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, Square and more to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct. Past sales can be imported.

For accountants: choose Per transaction (each sale with SKU and customer) or Summary (one journal entry per day, month or payout).

Sales post themselves

Fees, refunds and taxes are split out and categorized by the rules you set once.

Rules match on amount, vendor, description or location and set accounts, classes and tax codes.

Payouts match, or you get a flag

Synder compares platform payouts with your ledger and sets aside differences.

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes go through a clearing account. Every reconciled period is logged for audit.

Synder RevRec spreads each payment over the months it covers and follows upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

Questions, answered.

Is my data safe?

Synder is SOC 2 Type 2 certified, with end-to-end encryption. See the Trust Report.

What is the difference between Per transaction and Summary?

Per transaction posts each sale with SKU and customer. Summary posts one journal entry per day, month or payout.

Which platforms and accounting tools work with Synder?

Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, Square and 30+ more, connected to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct.

Do I still review anything?

Yes, but only exceptions. Synder posts what matches and flags what does not.

I run an accounting firm. Is there a partner program?

Yes. Firms get partner perks and a single workflow for client books.

Do you offer Synder Insights?

Yes. Synder Insights puts sales, product and customer reports for every platform on one dashboard.
