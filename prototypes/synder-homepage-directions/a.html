How a month goes with Synder

1

Connect your channels and your ledger

Link Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, Square and 30+ other platforms, then pick QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct. Past sales can be imported too.

Accountants: choose Per transaction (each sale with SKU and customer) or Summary (one journal entry per day, month or payout).

Picture: Diagram: platform logos on the left, accounting software logos on the right, a single connect button between them
2

Watch every sale post itself

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes land as the right entries, in the right currency, with the right category. Rules you set once decide which account each transaction belongs in.

Rules match on amount, vendor, description or location and assign accounts, classes and tax codes.

Picture: Screen: a list of synced transactions, each with a green posted tag and a category chip
3

Check that the books match the payouts

Synder compares what each platform says it paid with what is in your ledger, and sets aside anything that does not line up, before month end.

Each sale, fee, refund and tax goes through a clearing account, which should reach zero when the payout hits the bank. Every reconciled period is logged for audit.

Picture: Diagram: platform payout on one side, ledger balance on the other, one mismatch highlighted in amber
4

Close the month, and count subscriptions properly

For subscription businesses, Synder RevRec spreads each payment across the months it was earned and adjusts for upgrades, cancellations and prorations.

RevRec builds ASC 606 recognition schedules from Stripe data or an Excel upload.

Picture: Chart: one annual payment spread across twelve monthly bars, with an upgrade recalculating the remaining months
