03 How it works

Four jobs, done in the background.

Picture: Diagram: platforms to ledger
Sync

Sales, fees, refunds and taxes from Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, Square and more go to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct.

Picture: Screen: matched and flagged payouts
Reconcile

Payouts are compared with your ledger. Differences are flagged before month end and each reconciled period is logged.

Picture: Screen: if-then rules
Categorize

Rules by amount, vendor, description or location assign accounts, classes and tax codes.

Picture: Chart: monthly recognition
Recognize

RevRec builds ASC 606 recognition schedules from Stripe data or an Excel upload.

Also: Synder Insights (sales, product and customer reports per platform) and customization (rename line items, pull in metadata, remove taxes, custom mapping).
